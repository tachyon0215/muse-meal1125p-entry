# muse-meal1125p-entry

Entry for the make.fun competition [The Best Meal You Ever Had](https://make.fun/bounty/u-pangg7-the-best-meal-you-ever-had-a3ac0f85).

## The entry

> My grandmother's miso soup, eaten at her low table the winter I turned twelve, when the steam fogged my glasses and she pretended not to notice I had seconds three times.

Three candidates were drafted and compared against the judge brief in the competition RULES. Selection reason: one sentence with a place, an age, steam on glasses and the quiet detail of unmentioned thirds - concrete, personal and memorable; the other candidates read as a snack or lacked a personal stake.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only, no network, deterministic) writes the fixed text to `entry.txt` (sha256 a6012bf801a5...).
